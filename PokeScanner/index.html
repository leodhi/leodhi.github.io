    Each person has their own cards and their own recent scans. This phone remembers the name you pick.

    Gemini key for scanning and eBay & Google prices

    Card pictures, sets and store prices come straight off the internet and need no key. Gemini reads a scanned card and does the "Check eBay & Google" search. Free keys come from aistudio.google.com — no charge, no card on file. If you already saved one in the LLMs app, it's used automatically. Only the account that set the app up can see or change keys; everyone in the family can use them.

    Groq key — free backup for scanning

    When Gemini's free allowance is used up for the day, Groq reads the card instead. About 14,000 free reads a day. It can't search eBay or Google.

    ChatGPT — paid backup for scanning

    Used only when Gemini and Groq both can't. It costs money — a penny or two a card — so each family member needs your say-so, below. If you already saved a ChatGPT key in the LLMs app, it's used automatically.
